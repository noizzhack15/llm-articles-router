* **Field of Interest** — what this group is generally oriented toward
* **Members** — individuals from your original list who best fit that function

## **Level 3 — Divisions**

**Definition:** Collections of Level 2 Groups.
Each defined by:
* **Groups (which Level-2 groups belong inside the division)**
* **Primary Occupation (the overarching functional identity of the division)**
* **Combined Field of Interest (merged from the member groups)**

**Examples:**

## **1. Science, Technology & Engineering Division**

**Groups Included:**
* Data & Engineering Group
* Creative & Design Group

**Primary Occupation:** Innovative Technical & Creative Development
**Combined Field of Interest:** Data analytics, engineering innovation, system design, architectural planning, creative visualization, brand and product development.

## **Level 4 — Companies**

**Definition:** Highest governing unit.
Each defined by:
* **Divisions Included**
* **Primary Occupation** (the overarching organizational identity)
* **Total Fields of Interest** (all fields combined from the divisions inside the company)

# **Classification Rules**

Follow these rules **in order**, using only the schema.

### **Rule 1 — Match by Field of Interest**
Determine the topic (Politics, Economy, Sports, Education, Health, etc.).
Assign only to recipients whose **Field of Interest** matches.

### **Rule 2 — Choose the Lowest Level That Fits**
* If content is about an individual → assign to Level 1.
* If content is about a group → assign to Level 2.
* If content is broad within a division → Level 3.
* If content spans multiple companies → Level 4.

### **Rule 3 — Escalate Upward Only When Necessary**
Move *up* only when no lower unit is a valid match.

### **Rule 4 — Maintain Structural Consistency**
* No invented individuals, groups, or divisions.
* No jumping sideways or breaking hierarchy.

### **Rule 5 — Avoid Ambiguity**
If multiple matches exist:
Pick the **smallest unit** with the **closest relevance**.

---

# **Required Output Format**
Produce exactly three fields:

### **1. Recipients**
* One or more **dot-separated hierarchical paths**, e.g.:
  `Company B. Business Division. Finance & Legal Group. Laura Becker — Attorney`
* OR: `Unclassified`
* OR: `Out-of-Scope`

### **2. Justification**
* Short, direct reasoning.
* Reference **only** schema facts + rules.

### **3. Confidence**
* A numeric score (e.g., `0.94`).
